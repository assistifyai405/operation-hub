# KVK readiness checklist (Sprint 28)

**Product / business information checklist — not legal advice.**

Use this before the owner proceeds with KVK registration. Mark items only when the
owner has decided or prepared the information (placeholders in the app are OK until then).

## Checklist

- [ ] Final trade / product name chosen
- [ ] Business activity description prepared (see suggestion below)
- [ ] Business contact email
- [ ] Domain decision
- [ ] Pricing direction (Stripe intentionally pending)
- [ ] Target customer description
- [ ] Privacy / Terms placeholders ready for real company info
- [ ] Invoice company information placeholders identified
- [ ] Stripe intentionally pending until business setup
- [ ] Closed beta technically ready (see [`CLOSED_BETA_CHECKLIST.md`](./CLOSED_BETA_CHECKLIST.md))

## Suggested concise description (owner review)

Assistify is an AI-powered business operating system for small businesses and freelancers.
It helps them manage clients, projects, tasks, AI-assisted work, proposals, contracts,
invoices, and everyday business workflow in one workspace.

## Explicitly out of scope for this checklist

- Choosing or claiming an SBI code (verify externally)
- Final counsel-reviewed Privacy / Terms text
- Production deployment
- Stripe go-live
